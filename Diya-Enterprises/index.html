About Us
The Construction of
New Generation

Mr. D. L. Patel founded Diya Enterprises in 1999, arriving in Mumbai with a vision to build excellence into every structure. Over 25 years, that commitment has grown into a company trusted by some of the city's most renowned builders.

We believe quality is never an accident — it is the result of honest intention, sincere effort, intelligent direction, and skillful execution. Our rich experience across varied projects in diverse conditions makes us the ideal partner for every kind of construction challenge.

Today, we partner with landmark builders like Hiranandani, Nirmal Lifestyle, Kanakia, Ashar Group, R R Developers, and Paradigm Realty, delivering projects that stand as a testament to our standards.
